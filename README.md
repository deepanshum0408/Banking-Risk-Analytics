📊 Banking Risk Analytics Dashboard
🔹 Project Overview

This project focuses on developing a Power BI dashboard for risk analytics in banking and financial services. The primary objective is to help minimize potential money loss from customer lending by leveraging data visualization and actionable insights.

The solution integrates banking client data, financial metrics, and risk indicators into an interactive dashboard that enables decision-makers to identify high-risk segments and improve lending strategies.

🔹 Problem Statement

Banks face challenges in lending decisions due to incomplete risk assessment of customers. Poorly informed lending increases the chances of defaults, leading to financial losses.

Objective:

To develop a dashboard that consolidates customer and financial data, providing a clear view of risks associated with loans, deposits, and credit, thereby aiding in effective decision-making.

🔹 Data Sources

The project uses multiple datasets (~14 files) including:

clients.csv / Banking.csv / Banking.xlsx → Customer demographics, financial accounts, loans, deposits.

Derived metrics for risk weighting, loan portfolio analysis, and customer segmentation.

Key Data Points:

Client demographics (Age, Nationality, Occupation, Income, Loyalty Tier)

Banking details (Deposits, Loans, Credit Cards, Balances)

Risk weighting and lending profiles

🔹 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard provides:

Overall Banking Metrics

Total Clients, Total Loans, Total Deposits

Loan Analysis

Home Loan, Business Lending, Credit Balances

Risk-weighted loan portfolios

Deposit Analysis

Checking, Saving, Foreign Currency Accounts

Deposit growth & patterns

Customer Segmentation

Loyalty tiers (Jade, Gold, Platinum)

Risk classification and financial behaviors

🔹 Project Workflow

Data Collection & Cleaning – Consolidated multiple CSV/Excel files into structured formats.

Metric Definition – Created KPIs such as Loan-to-Deposit Ratio, Risk Weighting, and Customer Loyalty Indicators.

Dashboard Development – Designed visual modules in Power BI for loans, deposits, and customer insights.

Documentation & Presentation – Compiled Banking Report.docx and Banking.pptx for clear stakeholder communication.

🔹 Outcomes

Delivered a comprehensive dashboard to monitor client financial activities and risks.

Helped stakeholders identify high-risk clients for proactive measures in lending.

Improved decision-making efficiency by centralizing key banking metrics.
